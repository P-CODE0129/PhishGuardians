Quick Safety Tips

Verify Emails

Check sender addresses carefully before clicking links or attachments.

Don't Share OTPs

Never share OTPs or CVV numbers with anyone claiming to be from your bank.

Inspect Links

Hover over links to see the actual URL before clicking.

Verify Callers

Always call back on official numbers to verify unexpected calls.
