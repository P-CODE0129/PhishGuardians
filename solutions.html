Comprehensive Protection Guides

Banking & ATM Safety Guide

Call Safety
  • Banks never call to ask for OTPs/CVV
  • Never share card details over phone
  • Hang up and call official bank number
SMS Alerts
  • Check sender ID (should be bank's official ID)
  • Never click links in unexpected messages
  • Report suspicious SMS to your bank
ATM Safety
  • Cover keypad when entering PIN
  • Check for skimming devices
  • Immediately report lost cards

UPI Transaction Safety

Verify Before Paying
  • Always confirm recipient name before sending
  • Check last 3 digits of registered mobile
  • Be wary of payment requests from unknown numbers
Security Settings
  • Enable biometric authentication
  • Set transaction limits
  • Use separate UPI PIN for different apps
Red Flags
  • Requests for "test transactions"
  • Payments for "unblocking" accounts
  • Refundable deposit requests

Avoiding Job Scams

Employer Verification
  • Check company website and LinkedIn
  • Verify email domain matches company
  • Search for reviews or scam reports
Payment Red Flags
  • Legitimate jobs don't ask for payment
  • Beware of "training kit" or "verification" fees
  • Never pay for background checks
Email Indicators
  • Generic greetings instead of your name
  • Poor grammar and spelling
  • Mismatched sender email and company

Safe Online Shopping

Delivery Scams
  • Only check order status on official site/app
  • Never share OTPs for "delivery verification"
  • Track shipments through retailer's portal
Website Safety
  • Look for HTTPS and padlock icon
  • Check domain name for misspellings
  • Avoid too-good-to-be-true deals
Refund Scams
  • Legitimate refunds go back to original payment
  • Never share account details for refunds
  • Beware of "extra payment" requests
