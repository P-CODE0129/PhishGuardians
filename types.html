Types of Phishing Attacks

Learn about different phishing techniques used by scammers to steal your information

Email Phishing

Fake emails that appear to be from trusted sources are used to steal personal information. These emails often contain urgent requests or enticing offers.

Warning Signs:

  • Generic greetings like "Dear Customer"
  • Poor spelling and grammar
  • Suspicious attachments or links

Spear Phishing

Targeted attacks aimed at specific individuals or organizations with personalized messages. These are harder to detect due to the detailed information used.

Warning Signs:

  • Uses your name and personal details
  • Appears to come from someone you know
  • Requests sensitive information

Smishing

Fraudulent messages sent via SMS to trick users into clicking harmful links. Often claim to be from your bank or delivery company.

Warning Signs:

  • Unexpected text messages about account issues
  • Shortened or suspicious links
  • Requests for immediate action

Vishing

Scammers impersonate officials or companies over phone calls to extract sensitive data. They create a sense of urgency to get immediate responses.

Warning Signs:

  • Caller claims to be from a government agency
  • Threatens legal action if you don't comply
  • Asks for OTPs, passwords, or banking details

Phishing Type Comparison

Type Channel Target Common Scenarios
Email Phishing Email Mass audience Fake invoices, account verification, prize notifications
Spear Phishing Email/Social Media Specific individuals Personalized requests from "colleagues", HR notifications
Smishing SMS/WhatsApp Mobile users Package delivery issues, bank account suspensions
Vishing Phone calls All phone users KYC updates, tax refunds, tech support scams
