Real Phishing Case Studies

Learn from real victims how phishing scams unfold and how to protect yourself

RP

Ram Prasad Sharma

52 years • Middle class employee • Jaipur

Amount Lost

₹1.2 Lakh

Recovery

₹20,000

Method

Vishing (Phone Call)

Incident Overview:

Ram Prasad Sharma received a call from a person claiming to be a State Bank of India (SBI) officer. The caller informed him that his ATM card would be blocked if he didn't update his KYC (Know Your Customer) details immediately.

How the Scam Unfolded:

1. Fake Urgency

The caller said, "Sir, your ATM will stop working by midnight if you don't verify your account."

2. Trust Manipulation

The scammer gave a fake employee ID and official-sounding language to sound legitimate.

3. Phishing Trap

The scammer asked for Mr. Sharma's debit card number, CVV, and OTP to "verify" his details.

4. Transaction Executed

Within 10 minutes, ₹1.2 lakh was transferred from his account through multiple transactions.

5. Bank Helpline Delay

When Sharma called SBI customer care, they informed him that it was a scam and suggested he report it to the cybercrime portal and police.

Aftermath:

Filed a Complaint

He visited the local police station and filed an FIR.

Minimal Recovery

The bank managed to block ₹20,000, but the remaining amount was lost.

Psychological Impact

Sharma felt humiliated and hesitant about using digital banking in the future.

Key Takeaways:

  • Banks never ask for OTPs or CVV numbers over phone
  • Always verify unexpected calls by contacting the bank directly
  • Never share sensitive information with unsolicited callers
AN

Ananya

22 years • Unemployed • Middle Class

Amount Lost

₹7,500

Recovery

₹0

Method

Email Phishing

Incident Overview:

Ananya received an email from a recruitment agency offering a high-paying remote job at a well-known company. The email looked official, with a company logo, professional language, and a job description matching her skills.

How the Scam Unfolded:

1. Fake Job Listing

The job description matched her profile, making it seem real.

2. Sense of Urgency

She was told she had to make the payment within 24 hours to secure the offer.

3. Multiple Payments

After the first deposit of ₹2,500 for a "training kit", she was asked to pay an additional ₹5,000 for "background verification."

4. Ghosting

Once she paid, the recruiter stopped responding, and she realized it was a scam.

Key Vulnerabilities Exploited:

Quick Decision Under Pressure
Lack of Verification
UPI Payments Without Caution

Protection Tips:

  • Genuine employers never ask for payment during recruitment
  • Always research companies before applying or paying any fees
  • Verify job offers through official company websites or contacts
FA

Faizi Ali

19 years • College Student

Amount Lost

₹1,000

Recovery

₹0

Method

Smishing (OTP Fraud)

Incident Overview:

Faizi Ali fell victim to an online scam after ordering a product that faced unexpected delays. A scammer posing as a delivery representative contacted him, claiming that his parcel had been canceled and needed verification to be redelivered.

How the Scam Unfolded:

1. Delivery Delay

The product Faizi ordered faced unexpected shipping delays.

2. Scam Call

A scammer called posing as a delivery agent, saying the parcel was canceled and needed verification.

3. OTP Request

The scammer instructed Faizi to share an OTP he received on his phone.

4. Money Debited

₹1,000 was immediately deducted from his account after sharing the OTP.

Lessons Learned:

Verify Orders Securely

Always check order status via official websites/apps only

Identify Scam Calls

Avoid sharing details with unknown callers; verify claims directly

OTP Awareness

Never share OTPs; they authorize transactions, not verification

What To Do If This Happens:

  1. Immediately contact your bank to block further transactions
  2. Change your online banking passwords
  3. Report the incident to the cybercrime portal (cybercrime.gov.in)
  4. Inform the e-commerce platform about the fraudulent call
